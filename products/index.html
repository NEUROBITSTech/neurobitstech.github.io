MetaboTest

MetaboTest – a 99% accurate fast test for multiple sclerosis based on metabolomics.

This test helps confirm multiple sclerosis. If you have previously undergone brain MRI screening and have been diagnosed with the disease, it can also be used as an additional test to monitor recovery and disease progression.

The test was developed using metabolite profiles obtained from a targeted metabolomics platform and predicts outcomes in multiple sclerosis, a neurological disease.
