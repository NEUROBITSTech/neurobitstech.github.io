About us

  • CEO of the company: Dr. sc. Andrea Komljenovic

    Andrea Komljenovic (born in Swiss-German part of Switzerland; 37 years old; Andrea Komljenović, she/her, she is swiss-born Croatian/Russian) is well-known for her bioinformatics and biomedical research where she developed software and bioinformatics methods for sequencing methodologies to approach neurological disorders. Moreover, her methods are sequencing platform-related, e.g. methylation array, RNA-seq, single-cell and spatial -omics. Andrea Komljenovic has done pioneering and ground-breaking work in bioinformatics, her research leads to new biomedical discoveries in neuroscience and aging, and developments in bioinformatics field.

  • The rest of the team is from ETH and University of Zurich, with further research experience from Cornell, USA.
