About us

CEO of the company: Dr. sc. Andrea Komljenovic

Andrea Komljenovic (born in the Swiss-German part of Switzerland; 37 years old; Andrea Komljenović, she/her, Swiss-born Croatian/Russian) is well known for her bioinformatics and biomedical research, where she developed software and bioinformatics methods for sequencing methodologies to approach neurological disorders. Moreover, her methods are sequencing platform-related, including methylation arrays, RNA-seq, single-cell, and spatial -omics. Andrea Komljenovic has done pioneering work in bioinformatics, leading to new biomedical discoveries in neuroscience and aging, as well as developments in the field of bioinformatics.

Andrea holds PhD from Switzerland (ETH/UZH and UNIL) and was working in the Swiss Nobel Prize winner team during her doctorate. The rest of the team is from ETH Zurich and the University of Zurich, with additional research experience from Cornell University, USA.
