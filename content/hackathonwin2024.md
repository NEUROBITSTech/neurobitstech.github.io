Our CEO Andrea won the Hackathon in 2024 🏆

Fresh off from reviewing and finishing the publication for Nature journals, our CEO Andrea went for a hackathon in France during 2024. 
The purpose of the hackathon was to give the researchers a chance to test their skills in programming in a relaxed and creative environment 
far away from publishing and review process. 

The hackathon had three main tracks dedicated to Bioinformatics for Genomics projects, along with a number of smaller tasks. 
A total of 15 teams participated in the event. The main tracks were open to teams of developers and the smaller tasks were for individuals. 
Winners were selected for each track by jury made of industry experts and others. 

Our CEO Andrea Komljenovic performed the best on the smaller tasks for individuals and she won a first place 🥇 🏆 . CONGRATULATIONS TO HER! 🎉
